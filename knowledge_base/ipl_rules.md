# IPL playing rules (summary)
_Starter summary; verify against the current season's IPL playing conditions._

## Overs and bowlers
A T20 innings has 20 overs and no bowler may bowl more than 4 of them.

## Powerplay
The first 6 overs are the powerplay: only two fielders may be outside the 30-yard circle.

## Impact Player
Each side names substitute players at the toss and may bring one on as an "Impact Player" during the match,
replacing a member of the XI. The replaced player takes no further part. There are cut-off points for the
substitution, so check the current season's conditions for the exact over.

## Tie-breaks and reviews
Tied league matches are settled by a Super Over. Reviews may also be used for wides and no-balls in
recent seasons.
