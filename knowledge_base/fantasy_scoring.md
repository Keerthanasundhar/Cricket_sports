# Fantasy scoring (T20, commonly used scheme)
_Starter summary; platforms differ. Confirm on your league's rules page._

## Batting
1 point per run, +1 per boundary four, +2 per six. Bonuses: +4 for 30 runs, +8 for 50, +16 for 100.
A dismissal for a duck costs 2 points (batters, wicketkeepers and all-rounders).

## Bowling
25 points per wicket (excluding run outs), +8 bonus for bowled or LBW dismissals, +8 for a 4-wicket
haul and +16 for 5 wickets. Dot balls score 1 point each. Maiden overs score bonus points.

## Team rules (typical)
11 players, 100 credits, at most 7 players from one team, and role limits: 1-4 wicketkeepers,
3-6 batters, 1-4 all-rounders, 3-6 bowlers. Captain earns 2x points and vice-captain 1.5x.
